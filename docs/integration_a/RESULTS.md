## Measurement and safety limits

GL's prior independent analytic/CGAL study validates that frozen detector family;
R2/R3 agreement alone is not an independent oracle. Current CJM outputs are checked
by GL, CJM checked volume/Boolean operations and measured world bounds. They have
not received a new CGAL run. CGAL is never a product runtime dependency.

Caps are 512 triangles per part, 200000 scanned pairs, cooperative 10 seconds per
detector call, baseline plus at most three candidates. Cancellation occurs between
trials; it does not preempt an individual Blender Boolean or rational intersection.
Failure or missing measurements cannot be accepted, and failed cleanup/restoration
blocks the scene session. The tested rollback injection is a controlled callback
failure, not proof of recovery from process termination, disk corruption or crashes.

Existing meshes/transforms, datablock inventory, selection/active object, mode,
cursor and unit scale are observed and protected for this synthetic path. This is
not a universal scene/UV/material/animation backup system. Inter-part zero-pose
overlap is distinct from continuous collision, articulation range and printing.
No real asset, mechanical strength, material fit or printer tolerance is certified.
