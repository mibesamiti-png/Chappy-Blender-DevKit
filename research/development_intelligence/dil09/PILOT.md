Status: READY FOR A LIMITED REHEARSAL; actual ABCD trial not authorized or executed.
Goal: demonstrate fewer unnecessary reads and user interventions, not another research theme.

## Scope and stop

Use a single already-approved, narrowly scoped task with a known finish condition. Prefer a DIL-owned research task for initial operational rehearsal; ABCD/GL adoption requires the relevant owner and A approval. Do not create an extra official assignment or request a separate review for every step. Do not start an unrelated feature after the finish gate. Stop on missing formal rules, contradictory task scope, stale head, unclear permissions, failed mandatory checks or an unavailable required runtime. Follow existing official policy even where it requires more data than this prototype.

## One-turn procedure

1. Record task ID, approved scope, starting HEAD, current authoritative assignment/dispatch and stop conditions. Resume checkpoints are historical hints only; refresh live facts and read required formal sources and relevant code/tests.
2. Use exact-context CI family summaries first. Confirm all independently required job-level evidence under existing official rules. For previously verified identical evidence reuse references where permitted; for failures retrieve only the relevant job summary then needed log lines. Do not infer success from a green family alone.
3. Execute related changes and tests in one bounded batch. Do not expand owner scope, skip required CI, or merge without authority.
4. Record final HEAD, changed paths, exact-head CI links, actual outcomes, untested cases and next task. Persist a short checkpoint with authoritative source references; if CI is pending, mark pending.
5. Measure actual user prompts, tool reads by category, log reads, CI runs, task start/end and explicit human intervention when observable. Record unavailable values as null. Compare to a similar historical completed task only if scope and required checks are comparable; do not attribute causal speedup to a single uncontrolled comparison.

## Acceptance

Mandatory constraints and per-case evidence all retained; zero stale evidence adoption, unauthorized edits and skipped mandatory checks. No normal green log fetch unless required for specific evidence; report and explain any exception. A smaller checkpoint alone is not an operational benefit. Compare recorded work before extending beyond a single task. Do not deploy or open additional research cycles if results show no practical benefit.
