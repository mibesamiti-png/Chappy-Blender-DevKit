# Topology Intelligence — phase 02

Private reference reanalysis performed locally; no reference geometry, source-identifying information, measurements or images included here. Distinguish an edge loop (opposite incident edges at an interior valence-four vertex) from an edge ring (opposite edges across a quad). Strict tracing excludes vertices touching non-quads; relaxed tracing permits a cyclic four-neighbor link even when incident faces are not all quads. Track boundaries, poles and non-quad stops separately. A path is a connected component of the edge continuation graph; closed paths have degree two throughout. Closed-loop counts are not quality scores. Boundary vertices are intentionally treated as stops in this metric.

Three independent synthetic tube fixtures (8 vertices per ring; 6 rings initially) checked expected invariants locally: baseline 48 vertices, 32 interior valence-four vertices and 4 strict closed loops; insertion of a complete ring gives 56 vertices, 40 interior valence-four vertices and 5 closed loops; splitting one interior quad into two triangles without adding vertices yields 2 interior valence-five vertices and 2 strict closed loops. All three fixture assertions passed. These examples establish that loop insertion can preserve pole counts and that arbitrary triangulation can interrupt loops; they do not establish deformation quality or intentional author decisions.
