# Applied 02 — editable synthetic eyewear

One independent synthetic sample, not a reconstruction of any private model. Goal: distinguish editable procedural construction from direct finished meshes. Use native right-rim quad strip + Mirror/Solidify/Bevel, independent lens mesh + Mirror/Solidify/Bevel, swept Bezier bridge, editable temple curves, hinge pivot empties and simplified cylindrical hinge details. No applied modifiers in deliverable. Units metres. Intended width about 140 mm, open temple length about 140 mm. Left and right temples independently rotatable around vertical local hinge axes; test open and each independently folded 85 degrees. This is a pose/parent-dependency test, not mechanical clearance or a functional hinge design. No personal assets or prior user-model measurements. 

Predeclared gate: <=600 base mesh vertices plus Bezier control points (count separately); all named rim/lens modifiers retained, native save/reopen and evaluated mesh signatures stable; each intended rotating part moves rigidly about expected pivot, unchanged non-descendants stay exact to 1e-6 m; rim and lens mirror reflection matches within 1e-6 m; per evaluated mesh independently check closed oriented edge incidence and CGAL self-intersection; no claim about contacts between separate components. Render open front and three-quarter, one folded three-quarter. Report evaluated triangles separately; no direct comparison of base and finished counts or visual superiority. UV preparation, collision-free folding, optical prescription, fitting and printable hardware out of scope. No DIL branch changes, no new organization, no product approval. Preserve failure and stop after bounded evaluation.
