# R14 — UV and normal diagnostics (retrospective, not a fresh holdout)

This round does not optimize geometry or UVs. It adds an independent, read-only audit of actual Blender-generated raw outputs from R11/R12/R13. Existing holdouts are now historical/seen data; no claim of new generalization. No private meshes. Do not suppress genuine overlap merely to pass CI.

## Method
- R12/R13: actual saved new-face corner UVs, not a regenerated cylindrical seed. Build UV-connected components by matching both corner UVs on a shared geometric edge within 1e-5. These are inferred UV islands, not Blender's explicit seam flags.
- Per-triangle signed UV area (absolute area <=1e-10 means near-degenerate at this corpus scale). Report both signs and mixed signs within connected islands; a whole mirrored island is not counted as mixed. This is a winding diagnostic, not a universal orientation or material-fidelity oracle.
- Map the local orthonormal 3D triangle basis to UV with a 2x2 Jacobian. Singular-value ratio measures local anisotropy, and square root of absolute determinant measures linear UV density. Exclude UV-degenerate faces from these statistics and count them separately.
- Use Shapely 2.1.2/GEOS STRtree and polygon intersection to count unordered triangle pairs with intersection area >1e-9 UV units squared. Edge contacts do not count. Also count >1e-6 as sensitivity analysis. Separate pairs in the same inferred island from cross-island pairs (intentional stacks may be valid). Pair-area sum is NOT geometric union area or percentage of atlas coverage. No wrapping, atlas tiling, or overlaps against source collar UVs assessed.
- Derive boundary face-normal angles from the actual saved joined XYZ triangle mesh; match strip vertices by float32 coordinates, reject ambiguity. Use R11 stored Blender boundary-angle maximum as cross-check, tolerance 1e-3 degrees. This measures geometric normals, not custom split normals/shader or rendered appearance.
- Nine analytic controls: clean square, 4x anisotropic stretch, folded same-island overlap, collapsed UV, stacked separate islands, translation invariance, whole-island mirror, flat boundary, right angle. Control failure or missing historical outputs fails audit execution. Observed defects do not fail the *diagnostic* tool. Product approval remains false regardless.
- Inputs: R11 28 outputs (normals only: no saved per-face-corner bridge UVs), R12 24 and R13 24 (UV+normals). Total 76 normal audits, 48 UV audits, from author-generated favorable synthetic collars only. No new fixture or new candidate. No source mutation. Local archived outputs measured on Python 3.13.5, NumPy 2.3.5, Shapely 2.1.2. New dedicated CI reuses successful R11/R12/R13 raw artifacts from its same run, with Python 3.12 and pinned audit dependencies.
